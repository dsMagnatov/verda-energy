# Site preferences

- Support mobile and desktop; preserve the established desktop composition.
- Never add arrow icons or arrow glyphs to buttons or navigation.
- Do not end headings with periods.
- Do not add text shadows or shadow scrims to garden story copy.
- Keep the interface minimal; avoid small decorative or explanatory copy.
- Preserve the Instrument Serif and Manrope font pairing.
- Keep scene-control panels and the Effects launcher hidden until the user asks to restore them; preserve the controls and shared settings.
